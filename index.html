01 — Why We Exist

Great animation has always needed a studio's budget. We built ours out of algorithms instead.

Our mission is to create world-class entertainment inspired by Africa's cultures, languages, traditions, and future — using the power of storytelling and artificial intelligence. Every short film, folktale, and soundtrack on this page was scripted, voiced, scored, and animated by a small team running an AI-native production pipeline. It's not a shortcut. It's a new way to build a studio.
